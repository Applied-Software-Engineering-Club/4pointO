Roadmap: Software Engineering, BS

The following roadmap is a sample advising map to complete the degree program in four years. Please consult your MyPlanner and major advisor as you develop your individualized academic plan. Students must have completed 60+ units in order to enroll in GE UD courses.

*Courses marked with an asterisk may be taken in a different semester than listed.

Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4

Year 1

Fall Semester (16 units)

  • CS 46A - Introduction to Programming  4 unit(s)
  • ENGR 10 - Introduction to Engineering  3 unit(s)
  • GE Area 1A  (Recommend ENGL 1A ) 3 unit(s)
  • GE Area 1C  3 unit(s)
  • MATH 30 - Calculus I  3 unit(s) (GE Area 2)

Spring Semester (14 units)

  • CS 46B - Introduction to Data Structures  4 unit(s)
  • ENGL 1B - Argument and Analysis  3 unit(s) (GE Area 3B)
  • MATH 42 - Discrete Mathematics  3 unit(s)
  • PHYS 50 - General Physics I: Mechanics  4 unit(s) (GE Areas 5A + 5C)

Year 2

Fall Semester (16 units)

  • Complete the Upper Division Writing - Directed Self Placement
  • GE Area 5B  3 unit(s)
  • CMPE 131 - Software Engineering I  3 unit(s) 
  • GE Area 3A  3 unit(s)*
  • GE Area 4  + US 1 or US 2-3  3 unit(s)*
  • MATH 31 - Calculus II  4 unit(s) (GE Area 2)

Spring Semester (15 units)

  • CS 146 - Data Structures and Algorithms  3 unit(s)
  • GE Area 6  3 unit(s)*
  • GE Area 4  + US 1 or US 2-3  3 unit(s)*
  • CMPE 70 Assembly Language Programming  3 unit(s) 
  • CMPE 165 Software Engineering Process Management  3 unit(s) 

Year 3

Fall Semester (15 units)

  • University Elective Lower or Upper Division 3 unit(s)
  • CMPE 120 - Computer Organization and Architecture  3 unit(s)
  • CS 149 Operating Systems  3 unit(s)
  • CS 157A - Introduction to Database Management Systems  3 unit(s)
  • CMPE 187 Software Quality Engineering  3 unit(s) 

Spring Semester (15 units)

  • Apply to Graduate
  • CMPE 148 - Computer Networks I  3 unit(s)
  • University Elective Lower or Upper Division 3 unit(s)
  • Additional Math/Science Electives 3 unit(s)
  • ENGR 100W - Engineering Reports  3 unit(s) (GE Upper Division Area 2/5 + WID)
  • MATH 33LA - Differential Equations and Linear Algebra  3 unit(s) OR MATH 142 Introduction to Combinatorics  3 unit(s)

Year 4

Fall Semester (16 units)

  • Additional Math/Science Electives 4 unit(s)
  • CMPE 172 - Large Scale Systems  3 unit(s)
  • MATH 161A Applied Probability and Statistics I  OR ISE 130 Engineering Probability and Statistics  3 unit(s)
  • CMPE 195A - Senior Design Project I  2 unit(s) (GE Upper Division Area 4)
  • CS 166 - Information Security  3 unit(s)
  • ENGR 195A - Global and Social Issues in Engineering  1 unit (GE Upper Division Area 4)

Spring Semester (13 units)

  • CMPE 195B - Senior Design Project II  3 unit(s) (GE Upper Division Area 3)
  • ENGR 195B - Global and Social Issues in Engineering  1 unit (GE Upper Division Area 3)
  • ISE 164 - Computer and Human Interaction  3 unit(s)
  • Technical Upper Division Elective 3 unit(s)
  • Technical Upper Division Elective 3 unit(s)

Roadmap Notes

*Courses marked with an asterisk may be taken in a different semester than listed.

Lower division courses should generally be taken in years 1-2, and upper division courses in years 3-4. Students must have completed 60+ units in order to enroll in Upper Division GE courses. Students should work with an academic advisor to create an individual roadmap using the MyPlanner tool.

  • A minimum 2.0 GPA in all required courses and technical electives taken at SJSU has been achieved.
  • A minimum grade of “C” in MATH 30 , MATH 31  and PHYS 50  has been achieved.
  • A minimum grade of “C-” in the remaining courses in preparation for the major as well as in all courses in requirements of the major is required.
  • A minimum grade of “C” in CMPE 195A /CMPE 195B  and ENGR 195A /ENGR 195B  has been achieved.

A minimum of 120 units is required for this degree.
